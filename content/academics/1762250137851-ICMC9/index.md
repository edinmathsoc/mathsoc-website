Register for the 9th Imperial–Cambridge Mathematics Competition! ICMC is coming to Edinburgh this year. ICMC is an undergraduate-level mathematics competition for all undergrads across Europe. Each year it is run as a two-round individual competition. Round One is held on the second to last Sunday of November, includes 6 problems over 3 hours. The top competitors are then invited to participate in Round Two, held on the last Sunday of February, which features 5 harder problems over 4 hours.

## ROUND 1
* Date: Sunday 23 Nov 2025, 13:00 - 16:00
* Venue: Lecture Theatre A , 40 George Square , The University of Edinburgh
 
## ROUND 2 (Top 100 contestants qualify from Round One)
* Date: Sunday 22 Feb 2026, 10:00–14:00.
* Location: Imperial College London

For those who wish to participate, please use the link above to fill the registration form. A preparatory session will be held in the weeks before the event which will go through past papers and offer general advice. Details about that session shall be provided to those who register.

For more information, please visit their website: https://icmathscomp.org/register/. If you have any questions, please contact the ICMC organiser at skumar5@ed.ac.uk .
