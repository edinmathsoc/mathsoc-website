## Jane Street Strategy & Product Internship Talk

Jane Street is hosting a Get to Know Strategy & Product session with MathSoc at the University of Edinburgh, and we'd love for you to attend!

Join us for an information session about our Strategy and Product Internship!

This role offers a unique opportunity to explore the intersection of technology, finance, and business strategy. You'll learn how we tackle challenging problems across departments, support the firm's growth, and gain a holistic perspective of our business.

### Thursday, 13th November

### 6:00pm - 7:30pm GMT

### University of Edinburgh (on campus)


This session focuses specifically on Strategy and Product. It will not cover Quantitative Trading, Quantitative Research, Software Engineering, or any other role.

We hope to see you there!
